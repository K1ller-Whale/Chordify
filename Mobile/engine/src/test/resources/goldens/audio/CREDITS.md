# Audio inputs

Every file here except `guitarset_rock1_comp.wav` is rendered by
`chordify_ai.export.mobile_goldens --render-audio` from `chordify_core.synth`.

`guitarset_rock1_comp.wav` is the first 30 s of the microphone recording of GuitarSet take
`00_Rock1-90-C#_comp` (`audio_mono-mic.zip`), with the samples unchanged. It was cut with
`--guitarset-take`.

> Qingyang Xi, Rachel M. Bittner, Johan Pauwels, Xuzhou Ye and Juan P. Bello, "GuitarSet: A
> Dataset for Guitar Transcription", 19th International Society for Music Information Retrieval
> Conference (ISMIR), 2018. https://zenodo.org/records/3371780
